Winning hearts through stomachs

Gastrodiplomacy is when a country uses its food to build friendships and reputation abroad. These are real campaigns and moments, and the dishes in your passport they helped make famous.

    Where the word comes from

    In 2002 The Economist described Thailand's plan to multiply Thai restaurants abroad as "gastro-diplomacy." Researchers now study it alongside cultural diplomacy and nation branding: a shared meal is often the first way people meet another culture.
